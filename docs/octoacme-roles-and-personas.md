# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Technical Leads

### Role Summary
Technical Leads guide technical direction and help the team make sound engineering decisions while supporting Developers in delivering the agreed product outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Guide architecture and technical design decisions
- Identify technical risks, constraints, and dependencies
- Support Developers with implementation questions and engineering practices
- Contribute technical estimates and explain trade-offs

### Goals
- Keep the solution secure, reliable, maintainable, and aligned with technical standards
- Make technical risks and trade-offs visible early

### Typical Communication
- Technical designs and architecture discussions
- Engineering planning and code reviews
- Risk and dependency updates

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with Developers on design, implementation approaches, and technical quality; Developers remain responsible for building and maintaining components.
- Advise Product Managers on feasibility and technical trade-offs so product decisions account for constraints.
- Share technical risks, dependencies, and estimates with Project Managers for planning and delivery coordination.

---

## Quality Assurance / Test Engineers

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance (QA) / Test Engineers help ensure that deliverables meet agreed requirements and quality expectations through systematic verification.

### Responsibilities
- Define test approaches and identify coverage needs
- Verify acceptance criteria and report defects or quality gaps
- Coordinate appropriate functional, integration, and regression testing
- Communicate quality risks and release-readiness evidence

### Goals
- Detect issues early and reduce avoidable production defects
- Make quality status and remaining risks clear to the team

### Typical Communication
- Test plans, test results, and defect reports
- Acceptance-criteria reviews and release-readiness discussions
- Quality-risk updates during delivery

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Collaborate with Developers to plan test coverage and reproduce or resolve defects; Developers remain responsible for code-level tests and fixing implementation issues.
- Confirm expected behavior and acceptance criteria with Product Managers.
- Report testing progress, unresolved defects, and readiness risks to Project Managers to inform delivery plans and status.

---

## UX Designers / User Researchers

### Role Summary
UX Designers / User Researchers help teams understand user needs and shape usable, accessible experiences through research and design.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and conduct user research or usability evaluation
- Translate user needs into flows, prototypes, and design guidance
- Identify usability and accessibility considerations
- Validate proposed experiences and communicate findings

### Goals
- Ensure solutions address real user needs and are understandable and usable
- Surface user-experience risks before they cause rework

### Typical Communication
- Research findings and user feedback
- User flows, prototypes, and design reviews
- Usability and accessibility findings

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with Product Managers to refine problem framing, prioritize user needs, and validate product outcomes.
- Collaborate with Developers to clarify designs and identify implementation constraints.
- Coordinate research and design dependencies with Project Managers so this work is reflected in project plans and timelines.

---

## Business or Domain Stakeholders / Decision Owners

### Role Summary
Business or Domain Stakeholders / Decision Owners provide subject-matter context and make or secure timely decisions for the areas they represent.

### Responsibilities
- Explain relevant business rules, operational needs, and domain constraints
- Make decisions within their delegated authority and identify the appropriate decision-maker when they cannot
- Review proposed outcomes for domain correctness and provide timely feedback
- Surface changes to business priorities, policies, or constraints

### Goals
- Ensure delivered outcomes meet business and domain needs
- Resolve domain questions and decisions without avoidable delays

### Typical Communication
- Requirements and domain clarifications
- Reviews, decision logs, and stakeholder briefings
- Escalations for decisions outside their authority

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with Product Managers to provide domain context and validate that product priorities and outcomes reflect business needs; Product Managers retain ownership of product prioritization.
- Provide Project Managers with decisions, decision owners, and timing constraints so plans and escalations remain current.
- Answer Developers' domain questions through agreed channels and review domain-sensitive behavior; Developers remain responsible for implementation.

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Roles may be combined or shared in smaller teams; the responsibilities and decision ownership should still be made explicit.
